# ADR 4. Gemini AI for Injury Reports Audit

## Context

Evaluating injury recovery timelines requires parsing medical information and comparing club estimations with general sports medicine parameters.

## Decision

We chose the Gemini AI API to automate the analysis. It processes historical injury data and current status, returning a comparative audit report containing recovery time estimates and medical context.

## Consequences

Clinical audit reports are generated dynamically in seconds. The AI's evaluation is parsed directly into structured data in the database and UI.
